# Demo video (90 seconds) and submission text

Record on **demo mode** (testnet, or the local sandbox with the +15m / +1h buttons). Keep every shot to what a judge can repeat.
Say the loop in the first 20 seconds: judges will match this to pump.fun, so show the meme *doing* something right away. Lead with the community and the fight between coins; idle burn is the cost of not showing up, not the headline.

## Open with the door

Start on the landing page and press **PLAY**. Let the stepper run (wallet made, funded, first bag bought, under a minute; speed it up
in the edit). It shows in one clip that anyone can try this with no wallet and no setup, and that the actions are real transactions
(the receipt links open on the NEAR explorer). Turn the sound on for the recording: the voucher chime on each buy and sell, and the sting before an Omen, land well on video.

> A memecoin whose holders play cards that rewrite its rules. Back an Inferno and the pot burns supply; call a Prophecy and the crowd
> bets on the chart; Rally your coin and raid a rival's pot. Cards cost Energy that the daily Ritual earns, and sleepers' bags burn
