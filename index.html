How it works

Three things a coin does together

A coin lives or dies by its community. Each step below is a real action in the app.

STEP 1

Show up

Say gm every 6 hours and lock tokens in the Vault. Go 24 hours silent and idle burn takes 1% of your unlocked tokens every 6 hours, never more than 8% a day.

STEP 2

Play cards together

Open two cards every 6 hours. Small cards fire alone. Big ones wait on the table until enough holders back or sign them.

STEP 3

Raid a rival

Three signers start a raid on another coin. Three rounds decide it, and the winner's pot takes 15% of the loser's.

Times shown are full scale. The demo runs 12 times faster, so a gm every 30 minutes and a Ritual day of 2 hours.

Raids

Win value from another coin, not from nowhere

A raid needs no card, only signers. The other coin can't decline it.

1

Sign

Three signers at level 2, five at level 3, eight at level 4. The defender gets 30 minutes to get ready.

2

Fill your plan

Put a Rally, Curse or Ward in each of three slots. Plans stay hidden until each round ends.

3

Three rounds

Each side scores buying, crowd size and locked tokens. Tactics then multiply the score by 1.1 or 0.9.

4

Settle

The winner's pot takes 15% of the loser's, or 20% on War Coins, and buys and burns its own tokens.

Questions

What people ask first

Is this real money?

No. It runs on NEAR testnet at demo speed. Testnet NEAR has no value, and a Ritual day takes two hours instead of 24.

Do I need a wallet?

No. Play free makes a practice wallet in your browser and sends it 0.25 testnet NEAR. You can also connect your own testnet wallet inside the app.

Can the team change the rules or take the money?

No. Every rule is fixed at launch, and the factory deletes its own access key in the same transaction that deploys it. The curve holds the NEAR buyers paid, and the creator's seed can't come back out.

Who are the bots?

Sixteen labelled bots keep the demo world moving by checking in, trading and starting raids. Their whole brain is one readable file in the repository, and their names carry a robot mark in the app.

What happens if I stop playing?

After 24 hours without a gm, idle burn takes 1% of your unlocked tokens every 6 hours, at most 8% in a day. Half burns and half goes to locked holders. Locked tokens never burn.

What decides a raid?

Each side scores buying, crowd size and locked tokens in three rounds, and hidden Rally, Curse and Ward cards tilt the score. The coin whose community shows up in bigger numbers has the edge.

Is it audited?

No. The contracts are not audited. They pass 56 tests at two scales and a random fuzz test, and the Judges page checks them on the chain. Card rolls use the block's random seed, and raid plans are readable onchain.

What is not built yet?

Fire against Stop contests, the Storm, Black Hole, Heist and Supernova cards, nine more Omens, a DEX pool for graduated coins and a card market. They are on the roadmap, not in the app.
